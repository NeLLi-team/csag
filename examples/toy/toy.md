# Toy Plastid Claim Manuscript

Bikash Example and Crysten Curator

## Abstract

Toy algae grown under blue light accumulated more plastid pigment than matched dark controls. The observation supports the claim that blue light increases plastid pigment accumulation in this toy system.

## Methods

Two toy algal cultures were grown for 24 hours. One culture received blue light and the matched control was kept in darkness.

## Results

Blue-light cultures measured 2.1 absorbance units of plastid pigment, while dark controls measured 1.0 absorbance unit.

## Discussion

These data suggest that blue light increases plastid pigment accumulation in the toy algal system. A larger replicate series would be needed to estimate the effect precisely.
